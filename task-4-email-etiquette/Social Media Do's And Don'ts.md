**Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist**

###  **\# Do’s**

* Think before you post – Take a moment before uploading any content that shows how your post might be interpreted by others. If it could be seen as offensive or inappropriate, it’s better not to post it.  
* Use clear and respectful language – Communicate in a way that reflects maturity even in casual conversations.  
* Protect your personal information – Be careful about sharing personal details like your location, contact information, or daily routines. It can be harmful if not taken seriously.  
* Engage in a positive way – Support others and contribute to meaningful, positive discussions.  
* Be mindful of your digital footprint – Remember that what you post today could still be visible to future employers or colleagues.

### **\# Don’ts**

* Don’t overshare – Posting too much personal information can put your safety and privacy at risk.  
* Don’t post when emotional – Posting while angry or upset can lead to regret and damage relationships or your reputation.  
* Don’t spread false information – Always check if something is accurate before sharing it with others.  
* Don’t engage in negativity or online conflicts – Arguing or insulting others online can reflect poorly on you.  
* Don’t assume anything is truly private – Even private accounts or messages can be shared or screenshotted.
