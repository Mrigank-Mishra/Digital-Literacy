**Prevention Checklist**

**Stay Safe Online**

* Use strong, unique passwords for every account and avoid reusing the same password across apps.  
* Turn on two-factor authentication for important accounts like email, banking, and social media.  
* Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or banking details with anyone.  
* Double-check UPI payment requests before approving.  
* Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions or logging into sensitive accounts.  
* Be cautious of links in emails, messages, or WhatsApp.  
* Download apps only from trusted sources like the Play Store or App Store, and check reviews before installing.  
* Log out of shared devices and avoid saving passwords on them.  
* Regularly update your phone and apps to stay protected.  
* Don’t scan random QR codes.

Report cybercrime at: National Cyber Crime Portal (https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
