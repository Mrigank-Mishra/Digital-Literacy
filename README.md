Project Overview
This repository contains the complete Digital Literacy Portfolio for the CSE0001 course. The work is framed around the role of a Student Digital Ambassador, with the objective of helping peers navigate the digital world safely and professionally. The repository is organized into five distinct tasks, each corresponding to a specific module of the course.

Module Summaries & Deliverables
Module 1: Create a Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
Summary: A one-page visual resource designed to help peers understand digital literacy and why it matters. The infographic covers key topics such as what digital literacy is, useful student tools, safe internet practices, online presence, and email etiquette.
Folder: task-1-presentation/
Module 2: Build Your Student Digital Portfolio
Summary: Setup of a foundational professional digital presence across key platforms checked by employers and the academic community. This includes created or updated profiles on platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Kaggle, Stack Overflow, or ResearchGate.
Folder: task-2-portfolio/
Module 3: Explore Coding & Collaboration Platforms
Summary: Familiarization with coding practice platforms (like HackerRank or CodeChef) and cloud collaboration tools to study and practice effectively. This module also includes the creation of a 'Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz' using Google Workspace.

Folder: task-3-platforms/
Module 4: Draft a Professional Email & Etiquette Guide
Summary: An exercise in professional online communication. This includes drafting formal emails for academic extensions and internship inquiries, alongside a comprehensive "Do's and Don'ts" checklist for responsible social media use.
Folder: task-4-email-etiquette/
Module 5: Cybercrime Awareness Case Study & Prevention Guide
Summary: A researched case study on a specific cybercrime threat (such as Phishing, UPI Fraud, or Identity Theft) targeting college students. It details how the crime happens and includes an actionable 'Stay Safe Online' prevention checklist.
Folder: task-5-cybercrime/
